KnowItWhere

Privacy policy

Your things stay yours.

Effective 24 September 2026

In short: KnowItWhere does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you save stays on your device.

What the app stores

The items you add (a name, a location and an optional photo) are stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. The app has no account, no servers, and makes no network requests.

Widget, Siri and Spotlight

To show your items in the home-screen widget, answer Siri, and appear in Spotlight search, the app shares item names and locations with those parts of iOS on your device. Photos are never shared with them. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; KnowItWhere receives only the item name you asked about and answers from data on your device.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a photo, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library. It only uses the photo you pick, stores a compressed copy on your device, and never uploads it.

Backups

Your items are included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, handled by Apple. KnowItWhere has no access to your backups.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not use third-party services.

Deleting your data

Delete an item in the app to remove it and its photo. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
