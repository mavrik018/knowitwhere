Ask Siri

Say “Hey Siri, where’s my passport in KnowItWhere?”, using the name you saved the item under. Siri answers out loud without opening the app.

You can also say “Hey Siri, find something in KnowItWhere”. Siri asks what you’re looking for.

If Siri doesn’t respond, open the app once after installing, then check that Siri is on in Settings → Siri.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for KnowItWhere, and choose a size. It shows the things you put away most recently. Tap one to open it.

Find items in Spotlight

Swipe down on your home screen and type an item’s name. Tap the result to open it in the app.

Frequently asked questions

Does my data sync between devices?
Not yet. Everything is stored on your iPhone and included in your normal iPhone backup.

Siri picked the wrong item.
When several items match, Siri answers with the most recently updated one. Give items distinct names, or say the full name.

How do I delete an item?
Swipe left on it in the list, or open it and tap the trash icon.
